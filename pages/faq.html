GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently Asked Questions

A few helpful answers before you place your order.

How do I choose the right flowers?

Think about the occasion, your preferred colours and the style you have in mind. You can browse the catalogue or try our Find Your Perfect Bloom quiz for inspiration.

Can I customise my bouquet?

Visit our Build a Bouquet page to explore the customisation option. Please check the available choices and contact Fatin's Florette if you need to confirm a particular flower or arrangement.

How can I check flower availability?

Flower availability may vary. Please check the catalogue and contact Fatin's Florette to confirm the availability of a specific flower or bouquet.

How do I ask about prices or my order?

Please visit our Contact Us page to enquire about current prices, ordering arrangements and any details you would like to confirm before ordering.

Do you offer flower workshops?

Fatin's Florette offers flower-related workshops. For the latest workshop dates, availability, fees and booking details, please contact the business directly.

Can I include personalised message card?

Yes, can. Printed card message is BND2 (no word limit). Handwritten card is Free Of Charge.
